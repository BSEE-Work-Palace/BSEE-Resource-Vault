About BSEE Resource Vault

BSEE Resource Vault is a student-led initiative of the BSEE 2025–29 batch, created with the aim of bringing essential Electrical Engineering resources together in one accessible and organized platform. The purpose of this initiative is simple: to make academic resources easier to find, easier to access, and more useful for students throughout their university journey.

The platform currently provides access to semester-wise study material, past papers, projects, and resources collected from senior batches. As our batch progresses through the degree, the platform will continue to grow with additional semesters, academic material, projects, and other resources that may benefit the students who come after us.

More than a collection of files and links, this platform represents a small effort by the BSEE 2025–29 batch to leave behind something useful for our juniors. We hope that the resources gathered here will save time, support preparation, encourage collaboration, and make the academic experience a little easier for those who follow.

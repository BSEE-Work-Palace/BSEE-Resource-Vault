About BSEE Resource Vault

BSEE Resource Vault is a student-led initiative of the BSEE 2025–29 batch, created with the purpose of bringing useful Electrical Engineering resources together in one organized and accessible platform. The idea behind the platform is simple: to make academic material easier to find, easier to access, and more useful for students throughout their university journey.

The platform currently provides access to semester resources, past papers, projects, and drives containing material collected from senior batches. As the BSEE 2025–29 batch progresses through the degree, the platform will continue to grow with additional semesters, academic resources, projects, and material that can help future students.

This platform is more than a collection of links. It is a small effort by the BSEE 2025–29 batch to create something useful for the juniors who will follow. We hope that the resources shared here will help students save time, prepare better, collaborate more effectively, and make their academic journey a little easier.
